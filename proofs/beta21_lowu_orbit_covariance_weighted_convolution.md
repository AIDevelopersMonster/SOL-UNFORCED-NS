## 3. Scalar convolution estimate

For two finitely supported complex sequences `a,b`, define

\[
c_r:=\sum_{j\in\mathbb Z}a_j\overline{b_{j-r}}.
\tag{CV3}
\]

Then

\[
\begin{aligned}
\sum_r w_r|c_r|
&\le
\sum_r\sum_j w_r|a_j||b_{j-r}|\\
&\le
\sum_r\sum_j w_jw_{j-r}|a_j||b_{j-r}|\\
&=
\left(\sum_jw_j|a_j|\right)
\left(\sum_kw_k|b_k|\right).
\end{aligned}
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\|a\star\widetilde b\|_{\ell^1_{\sigma_0}}
\le
\|a\|_{\ell^1_{\sigma_0}}
\|b\|_{\ell^1_{\sigma_0}}.
}
\tag{CV4}
\]

The convolution constant is exactly one.

No factor depending on the support cardinality appears.
