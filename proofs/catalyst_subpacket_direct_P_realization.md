They can be realized internally by splitting the already-present unit-beta catalyst `C` into four independently amplitude-tunable, time-localized subpackets of the same phase and polarization and interacting them with the fixed surviving unit-beta packet `D`.

## 1. Catalyst subdivision

Work on the first late beta-(2,1) gate, where the intended edge is

\[
C+D\longrightarrow P.
\tag{CR1}
\]

Let the incoming catalyst have phase `Phi_C`, unit signed beta, and principal growing polarization `g_C`. Let the surviving packet `D` have phase `Phi_D` and growing polarization `g_D`.

Choose four smooth compactly supported scalar profiles

\[

\tag{CR2}
\]

inside one source-safe collision interval `I`, with distinct translated centers `tau_j`. For complex amplitudes `p_j`, define the catalyst control field at coefficient level by

\[
\boxed{
C_{\rm ctrl}
:=
\sum_{j=1}^4 p_j C_j,
}
\tag{CR3}

The total catalyst is

\[
C=C_{\rm base}+C_{\rm ctrl}.
\tag{CR4}
\]

No external force has been added: the `p_j` are finite-dimensional parameters of the approximate oscillatory field itself.

## 2. No new lattice generator

Every `C_j` carries the same harmonic character as `C`. Therefore, for every `j`,

\[
\operatorname{char}(C_j)=\operatorname{char}(C).
\tag{CR5}
\]

Hence the finite control bank does not enlarge the two-generator lattice generated by the original parent/catalyst characters. In particular,

\[
\boxed{

}
\tag{CR6}

The four control parameters are amplitude/time-profile degrees of freedom, not new frequencies.

## 4. Same-phase catalyst controls do not create a new principal quadratic branch

The potentially dangerous new interaction is between two catalyst control copies. At principal WKB level all `C_j` have the same wave normal `k_C` and the same divergence-free polarization `a_C` satisfying

\[
k_C\cdot a_C=0.
\tag{CR14}
\]

For a plane wave `a_C e^{ik_C\cdot x}`, the principal convective self-interaction is proportional to

\[
(a_C\cdot k_C)a_C=0.
\tag{CR15}
\]

Hence for any two scalar multiples of the same phase/polarization,

\[
\boxed{
\Pi_{2C}^{\rm principal}
\bigl[
\mathcal B(C_i,C_j)+\mathcal B(C_j,C_i)
\bigr]
=0.
}
\tag{CR16}
\]

This applies also to `i=j`. Thus splitting `C` into four copies does not introduce an order-one principal `2C` control-control source.

The exact localized packets are not literal constant-amplitude plane waves. Derivatives of `chi_j`, slow frame variation and curl remainders therefore produce nonzero lower-order terms. Those terms are precisely of the source-localization types already controlled by Proposition 9.1/Lemma 7.7 in the branch: they carry polynomial `S_*` losses multiplied by a positive source epsilon gain and/or `O(S_*^{-1})` frame error. They belong to the correction forcing, not to a new order-one critical branch.
