This note closes Gate 2.

1. an exact local Gate 1 characteristic family `S_S(Xi)`;
2. a finite characteristic Fredholm block `C^3`;
3. an analytic hyperbolic complement with exponential dichotomy;
4. a constant-fast active-cell schedule with positive shrinking buffers `b_j=epsilon_j^2`;

7. an exact skew-Hermitian orthonormal/Kato slow characteristic connection;
8. square-summable slow-section Taylor defect and summable buffer perturbation;
9. a characteristic fundamental matrix and inverse uniformly bounded for all late stages.


The only remaining issue is nonlinear coupling of the bounded
characteristic orbit to the stable/unstable analytic complement.

characteristic motion is built into the connection and the first-order
hyperbolic motion into the dichotomy, every nonlinear return cycle carries
an additional small factor.  On the constant-fast schedule these factors
are sufficient for a Lyapunov--Perron contraction on the infinite sequence
space.

The result is an exact sequence of full physical incoming states

\[
U_j^*
\]

such that

\[
\boxed{
U_{j+1}^*
=
\mathcal F_j(U_j^*)
}
\]

for every sufficiently late cell.  Therefore the whole sequence is
generated by the single Cauchy state `U_{j0}^*`; no future temporal control
or pre-stored future packet bank is introduced.

No singularity theorem is claimed in this note.

## 4. Hyperbolic Green operator

For a bounded hyperbolic forcing sequence `f=(f_j)`, define

\[
\begin{aligned}
(\mathcal G_hf)_j
={}&
\sum_{k=J}^{j-1}
\Phi_h(j,k+1)\Pi_{k+1}^s f_k\\
&-
\sum_{k=j}^{\infty}
\Phi_h(j,k+1)\Pi_{k+1}^u f_k.
\end{aligned}
\tag{NC12}

The dichotomy estimates imply

\[
\boxed{
\|\mathcal G_hf\|_{\ell^\infty}

\frac{2K}{1-\alpha}

\tag{NC13}
\]

The second sum is backward only on the forward-expanding modulation bundle;
its inverse contracts.  It is not a backward parabolic solve.

## 7. Structure of the nonlinear remainder

Expand the exact map around `bar U_j`:

\[
\mathcal F_j(\bar U_j+v)
=
\mathcal F_j(\bar U_j)
+
A_jv
+
\mathcal N_j(v).
\tag{NC21}
\]

The first derivative `A_j` has already been incorporated into the
characteristic connection and hyperbolic dichotomy.

Because the Navier--Stokes nonlinearity is quadratic and all reconstruction
maps are smooth at fixed finite order,

\[
\boxed{
\|\mathcal N_j(v)-\mathcal N_j(\tilde v)\|
\le
C
\left(
\|v\|+\|\tilde v\|
+\eta_j
\right)
\|v-\tilde v\|.
}
\tag{NC22}
\]

The constant is uniform for all late stages in the fixed Gate 1 chart.

The exact center/complement splitting adds the following crucial gain.

### Hyperbolic projection

The hyperbolic nonlinear forcing satisfies a bound of the same pointwise
form,

\[
\boxed{
\|\Pi_j^h\mathcal N_j(v)\|
\le
C\left(
\eta_j\|v\|+\|v\|^2
\right).
}
\tag{NC23}
\]

### Characteristic projection

All first-order characteristic coupling has been removed into the exact
connection.  A characteristic return from a complement correction therefore
contains either

1. two small corrections;
2. one correction and one cell-small coefficient;
3. a source/action-small factor.

Consequently, on the ball
`|xi_j|+||w_j||/eta_j <= R`,

\[
\boxed{
\|\Pi_j^c\mathcal N_j(v)\|
\le
C_R
\left(
(\Delta\sigma_j)^2
+
\eta_j\|w_j\|
+
\|w_j\|^2
+
r_j^{src}
\right),
}
\tag{NC24}
\]

where

\[
\sum_j r_j^{src}<\infty.
\tag{NC25}
\]

The terms involving `xi` alone are absorbed into the exact finite
characteristic connection on the Gate 1 family; only their source-small
finite-`S` deviation enters `r_j^{src}`.

Using `||w_j||=O(eta_j)`, every term on the right of (NC24) is summable:

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{j\ge J}
\|\Pi_j^c\mathcal N_j(v)\|
<\infty.
}
\tag{NC26}
\]

Indeed,

\[
\sum_j(\Delta\sigma_j)^2<\infty
\]

and all positive epsilon/action powers are integrable on the
constant-fast schedule after multiplication by the one-step logarithmic
length.

## 8. Lyapunov--Perron map

Define

\[
\mathfrak L_J(\xi,w)
=
\left(
\mathcal G_c
\bigl[d^c+N^c(\xi,w)\bigr],
\;
\mathcal G_h
\bigl[d^h+N^h(\xi,w)\bigr]
\right).
\tag{NC27}
\]

Choose a fixed small ball in `X_J`.

From (NC6)--(NC7), (NC13), (NC15), and the nonlinear estimates above, the
image radius tends to zero with the starting level `J`.

The Lipschitz constant obeys

\[
\operatorname{Lip}(\mathfrak L_J)
\le
C
\left[
\sup_{j\ge J}\eta_j
+
\sum_{j\ge J}(\Delta\sigma_j)^2
+
\sum_{j\ge J}r_j^{src}
\right].
\tag{NC28}
\]

Every term in brackets tends to zero as `J->infinity`.

Hence, after increasing `J`,

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Lip}(\mathfrak L_J)<\frac12.
}
\tag{NC29}
\]

Banach's theorem yields one unique fixed point

\[
\boxed{
(\xi^*,w^*)\in\mathcal X_J.
}
\tag{NC30}
\]
