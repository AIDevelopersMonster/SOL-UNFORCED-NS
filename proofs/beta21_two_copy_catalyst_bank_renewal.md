# Two-copy catalyst-carrier bank renewal for the corrected beta-(2,1) cell

**Status:** PROVED FINITE SUPPORT-CARRIER RENEWAL THEOREM, CONDITIONAL ONLY ON THE ALREADY-STATED NUMERICAL/SOURCE-LINE CAVEATS OF THE INDIVIDUAL EXACT GATES. This theorem upgrades the one-cell finite-bank construction by showing that the two same-character catalyst carriers required by one cell can be regenerated forward by the previous cell with **no increase in support-bank cardinality**.

The theorem renews the physical catalyst carriers and their beta/slope/action/amplitude/phase data. It does **not** yet prove autonomous renewal of every localized control-profile degree of freedom used internally by the entrance, strong-`H`, and terminal Jacobians. That stronger finite-state theorem is the next obligation.

## 3. Route one child into the renewal circuit and reserve the other

After the entrance exits, route the two generated unit-beta packets differently.

### Active child

`D^A` follows the already proved finite-bank three-collar path. It meets the retained catalyst copy `C^B` on the late strong-`H` collar.

### Reserve child

`D^B` is routed into a support corridor disjoint from:

- `C^A` after its entrance exit;
- `C^B` before and during the strong-`H` supernode;
- the strong-`H` control packets;
- the post-strong-`H` `(P_new,D^A)` overlap;

The translated auxiliary-rectangle theorem permits this finite list of incidence/non-incidence requirements because the bank is finite and all supernode centers are prescribed before the generic separation step.

Thus `D^B` undergoes only homogeneous/source-small forward transport inside the same exact correction problem. It does not participate in the active renewal genealogy.

## 8. Exact correction across the doubled finite circuit

The doubled entrance, middle transport, active strong-`H` path, reserve-child path and late terminal gate still form a **fixed finite** designated circuit.

The argument of `two_collar_zero_residual_reduction.md` and `beta21_corrected_finite_bank_three_collar_cell.md` applies with only a larger finite design constant:

- finitely many additional entrance controls;
- finitely many additional support labels;

Thus the catalyst-carrier renewal is an exact finite-dimensional zero-residual theorem at sufficiently high levels, subject to the same branch-wide numerical/source-line caveats as the constituent local gates.

## 10. Remaining finite-state caveat: control-profile renewal

Each exact gate theorem is formulated using a finite family of localized same-character control subpackets:

- four `P` profiles per entrance gate;
- nine `C` profiles in the strong-`H` gate;
- three `P` profiles in the terminal gate;
- finite phase/amplitude parameters.

In the local PDE solve these are internal finite degrees of freedom of the approximate designated field. For a truly autonomous cross-scale Markov state, one must still prove one of the following:

1. the required control profiles can be generated as fixed localized decompositions of the renewed physical carriers `(P,C^A,C^B)` at the next scale, without introducing independently preloaded future packets; or
2. enlarge the renewed state by a **fixed finite control-profile bank** and prove that this full bank is returned with constant cardinality.

This is the next sharp support-state theorem.
