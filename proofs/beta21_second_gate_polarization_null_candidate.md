## 1. Reference polarizations

At source-frame slope `s`, use the transverse basis

\[

\qquad N,
\]

and write growing/decaying polarizations as

\[
g(s)=h(s)+q(s)N,
\qquad
d(s)=h(s)-q(s)N,
\qquad q(s)=\sqrt{1+s^2}.
\tag{PN1}
\]

The incoming beta-one packet `D` is kept in its growing polarization

\[
g_D=g(s_D).
\tag{PN2}

\[
\boxed{

\tag{PN3}

Equivalently,

\[
p_P(\lambda)
=(1+\lambda)h(s_P)+(1-\lambda)q(s_P)N.
\tag{PN4}
\]

The parameter `lambda` is order one at the frozen principal level.
