## Corrected preferred local formulation — forward nonzero-harmonic evolution

The preferred local architecture remains a forward input-output map, but `proofs/forward_relay_input_output_closure.md` contained two overstatements that are now corrected by

`proofs/nonzero_harmonic_forward_reduction.md` and `notes/mean_block_zero_force_audit.md`.

First, the mixed terms with the designated packets are **not** assumed to have a small operator norm. They are absorbed into the full linearized forward evolution

\[
\partial_v z=(\mathcal D+\mathcal K)z+f+\mathcal B(z,z),
\]

where `K` contains the designated parent/catalyst/child linearization. Parabolic high-mode smoothing and a Volterra argument on the bounded relay interval give a truncation-independent full propagator on the nonzero harmonic analytic lattice.

Second, the supported stage-zero wave defect is not purely exponentially flat. Conservatively, using the source stage-zero exponent `B_0=0.7` against the primary wave exponent `1/2`, the correct small parameter is

\[
\boxed{
\rho_\ell
\lesssim
S_*^C\varepsilon^{1/5}
+S_*^Ce^{-c_MS_*}.
}
\]

Both terms tend to zero as the dyadic level grows, but the first is only algebraically small.

For each sufficiently small angular mean input `m`, the full nonzero harmonic problem has a unique exact forward solution

\[
\boxed{z=z(m)}
\]

with norm `O(rho_ell)` in the analytic packet space. The nonzero velocity is constructed directly in the source curl-potential representation, so it remains real and divergence-free without a post-hoc projection.

## Angular mean — THE REMAINING LOCAL ZERO-FORCE BARRIER

The angular Fourier mode `0` is generated by products of every harmonic with its conjugate and is not part of the two-generator nonzero action-filter lattice.

`notes/mean_block_zero_force_audit.md` re-audits Section 8 of the OpenAI construction. The source mean system contains:

1. radial pressure/stress inversion with weighted radial moment conditions;
2. inversion of the zero auxiliary-torus-average part along the fast torus direction, with a fixed polynomial small-divisor derivative loss;
3. a five-dimensional moment correction that cancels the residual scalar defects `P`, `J_theta`, and `J_z` by an explicit Vandermonde-invertible map.

The five-dimensional rank is already source-certified and is not a new obstruction. The nonlinear recomputation has a strong exponent gain (source Lemma 8.8), schematically

\[
\boxed{
S^\alpha\longmapsto S^{\alpha+0.9-2\kappa_s}.
}
\]

However, the source only uses this gain at finite correction depth and permits derivative constants to depend on the stage. For an exact unforced local solution one still needs an **infinite mean correction theorem** that absorbs the fixed derivative loss of the auxiliary-torus inverse.

The full local equation is therefore reduced to

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal F_{\rm mean}(m;z(m))=0.
}
\]

The two plausible routes are:

- a tame `C^infty`/Nash--Moser iteration using torus Fourier smoothing;
- a compactly supported Gevrey-`s>1` scale, whose Fourier decay absorbs the polynomial divisor loss.

## Immediate frontier — tame mean inverse theorem

Construct a scale of mean spaces `Y_s` and an exact mean correction operator satisfying a tame estimate of the form

\[
\|G_{\rm mean}F\|_{Y_s}
\le C_s\|F\|_{Y_{s+r}}
\]

for one fixed derivative loss `r`, together with a nonlinear recomputation estimate

\[
\boxed{
\|\mathcal R_{\rm mean}(m)-\mathcal R_{\rm mean}(\widetilde m)\|_{Y_s}
\le
C_s\varepsilon^\delta
\|m-\widetilde m\|_{Y_{s+r}}
}
\]

for some `delta>0` independent of iteration depth.

A Nash--Moser or decreasing-Gevrey-radius theorem of this type would give exact local mean closure and then allow the nonzero forward reduction to produce the full local zero-force relay.
