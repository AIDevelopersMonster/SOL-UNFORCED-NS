### 3. Principal amplitude renewal has a nonzero transverse fixed ray

`proofs/principal_two_event_amplitude_fixed_ray.md` solves the finite algebraic recurrence. If the two normalized designated interaction coefficients are `kappa_1,kappa_2 !=0`, then

\[
D_1=\kappa_1A_2\overline{A_1},
\qquad
R_2=\kappa_2A_1D_1,
\]

and hence

\[
\mathscr R_0(A_2,A_1)
=(\kappa_1\kappa_2A_2|A_1|^2,
\kappa_1A_2\overline{A_1}).
\]

For every `r>0`,

\[
A_1=re^{i\phi},
\qquad
A_2=\kappa_2r^2e^{2i\phi}

\[
\lambda=\kappa_1\kappa_2r^2.

\[

\[

\]
