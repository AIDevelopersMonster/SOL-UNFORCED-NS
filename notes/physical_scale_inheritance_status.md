## Closed in this branch

### 1. Whole-space mean/pressure tail is not the cross-scale bottleneck

`proofs/previous_relay_mean_tail_subcriticality.md` proves that if

\[
q_{j+1}=\vartheta q_j,\qquad 0<\vartheta<1,
\]

and the single-relay mean correction satisfies

\[
\|m_j\|\lesssim S_j^C\varepsilon_j^{1-\kappa_s},
\qquad \varepsilon_j\asymp q_j^h,
\]

then, after restoring physical units and renormalizing by the next relay base scale,

\[
\boxed{
\frac{|m_j^{\rm phys}|}{U_{\rm base}(q_{j+1})}
\lesssim
S_j^C\vartheta^{1/2+h}\varepsilon_j^{1-\kappa_s}
\to0.
}
\]

At every fixed finite derivative order the inherited background is still `o(1)` in the next relay's normalized seminorm. The global pressure projector is order zero and does not remove the positive epsilon gain. Earlier relay tails are summable because exponential/geometric epsilon decay dominates the polynomial `S_j^C` factors.

Therefore strict open relay conditions (action gap, projection margin, critical-block transversality, coefficient cone conditions) survive the accumulated whole-space mean/pressure background from sufficiently high starting level onward.

### 2. Exact child gain requirement

`proofs/designated_child_cross_scale_gain_requirement.md` derives the physical primary packet amplitude

\[
U_{\rm pkt}(q)
\asymp q^{-A}\varepsilon(q)^{1/2}
=q^{-(1+h)/2},
\qquad A=1/2+h.
\]

The corrected physical carrier satisfies the same exponent:

\[
\Omega_{\rm phys}(q)\asymp q^{-(1+h)/2}.
\]

Hence a transition `q_j -> q_{j+1}=vartheta q_j` requires the designated child to acquire the single common gain

\[
\boxed{
\vartheta^{-(1+h)/2}
}
\]

in both physical packet amplitude and carrier frequency.

This cannot come from a dyadic chart change; `chart_invariant_carrier_scale.md` already proves chart invariance at fixed physical point. The gain must be generated by genuine q-evolution along the background characteristic.
